# **CRHE-Long-Term-PPG-BP-Dataset Overview**

## **The screening of elderly participants**

**The screening of elderly participants** in this study was conducted based on predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria to ensure both the quality of pulse wave signals and participant safety.

## A. Inclusion Criteria

Eligible participants were aged 60 years or older and voluntarily participated in the study. Informed consent was obtained from all participants; in cases of cognitive impairment, consent was provided by the participant’s legal guardian.

## B. Exclusion Criteria

Individuals were excluded if they met any of the following conditions, which could compromise signal quality or pose safety risks during the study:

- **Poor signal quality**: Pulse wave amplitude significantly below the normal range, unsuitable for reliable data analysis.
- **Uncontrolled hypertension**: Consistently elevated resting systolic blood pressure (SBP) exceeding 200 mmHg.
- **Finger abnormalities**: Limited joint mobility (e.g., severe arthritis), skin stiffness, or finger circumference > 8 cm, which may affect proper sensor attachment.
- **Device material allergy**: Known hypersensitivity to the photosensitive resin used in the sensor casing, with prior history of rash or pruritus.
- **Critical or unstable health conditions**: Including but not limited to acute myocardial infarction, stroke, or septic shock, which may pose health risks during participation.
